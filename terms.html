A plain-language guide to using Docket and how this browser-based tracker handles information.

Effective date: September 30, 2026

1. Using Docket

Docket is a personal tool for recording income and expenses, viewing summaries, and setting a spending budget. By using it, you agree to use it lawfully and to keep your device and browser reasonably secure.

You are responsible for the descriptions and amounts you enter, and for keeping any exported backup safe. Do not use the tracker to store passwords, full payment-card numbers, bank login details, government identifiers, or other information that should remain confidential.

2. Not financial advice

Docket is an organizational tool. It is not a bank, financial adviser, accountant, tax professional, or investment service. Its totals and charts are provided for general information and may contain errors if the information entered is incomplete or incorrect. Do not rely on the app alone when making financial, tax, investment, or legal decisions.

3. Where your data is stored

Transaction descriptions, amounts, categories, types, and dates are stored in your browser’s local storage on the device you are using. The monthly budget and selected currency are stored there as separate preferences. The app does not create an account or sync this information to a Docket server.

  • Local storage is not encrypted by this app. Anyone who can use your browser profile or device may be able to view the saved data.
  • Private browsing, browser storage limits, clearing site data, device loss, or browser problems can remove the data. Export a JSON or Excel backup if you need to keep a copy.
  • Exported files are not encrypted by the app. Store them somewhere you trust and delete them when no longer needed.
  • Use “Clear All” to remove transactions from the app. To also remove the saved currency and budget preference, clear this site’s local storage in your browser settings.

4. Optional location and currency lookup

Location is optional. The app requests browser location access only after you open currency settings and choose “Use my location.” If you allow access, the app sends the coordinates supplied by your browser to BigDataCloud to identify your country. It then sends the country code—not your coordinates—to REST Countries to find a currency. The app does not save your coordinates. You can deny or revoke location permission and select a currency manually instead.

Browser geolocation generally requires a secure context, usually HTTPS. Read the providers’ privacy information to understand their own processing of requests. Docket cannot control their services or policies.

5. Third-party resources

The tracker loads Chart.js from jsDelivr and fonts from Google Fonts. When you choose Excel export, it also loads the SheetJS library from its CDN. Your browser makes requests to those providers to load resources. The app creates the workbook in your browser and does not intentionally send transaction details in those requests. These services are subject to their own policies and availability. If a resource is unavailable, some features may not work.
